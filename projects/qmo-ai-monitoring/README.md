# QMO AI Monitoring & Analytics System

## Overview

An intelligent monitoring and analytics platform designed to support quality monitoring, KPI tracking, operational analytics, and enterprise-scale observability workflows.

The system combines analytics engineering, monitoring pipelines, and intelligent scoring approaches to improve operational visibility and service quality analysis.

---

## Problem Statement

Enterprise operational environments generate large volumes of monitoring and KPI data that require:
- Centralized analytics
- Intelligent monitoring
- Real-time KPI visibility
- Operational scoring
- Service quality tracking

Traditional monitoring systems often lack flexible analytics workflows and intelligent operational insights.

---

## Objectives

- Build scalable monitoring and analytics workflows
- Support KPI-driven operational visibility
- Enable intelligent quality monitoring
- Improve operational analytics capabilities
- Support scalable enterprise observability
